# Source — Olongapo City Municipal Climate Normals

- **File:** `olongapo_city_municipal_monthly.csv`
- **Provider:** PAGASA (Philippine Atmospheric, Geophysical and Astronomical Services Administration)
- **Product:** ClimGridPh / CliMap v2.0 — municipal climate normals
- **Coverage:** Rainfall normals 2001–2020; temperature normals 1991–2020
- **Scope:** Olongapo City, Zambales, Region III — municipal grid average
- **Rows:** 12 (one per calendar month)
- **Fields:** id, Month, Rainfall (mm), MaxTemp, MinTemp, MeanTemp (°C), Region, Province, Municipality
- **Retrieved:** 2026-09-10 (supplied by team via ClimGridPh download portal)

## Verified findings

| Month | Rainfall (mm) |
|---|---|
| **August** | **865.97** ← wettest month |
| July | 788.38 |
| September | 632.31 |
| June | 506.80 |

August leads July by 78 mm. This supports the storyboard's central claim (P6): August was
already Olongapo's wettest month across the 30-year normal, *before* the record 2026 season.
