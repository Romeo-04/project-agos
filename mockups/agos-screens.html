AGOS — interface mockups

Ten screens for the ASEAN DSE storyboard and finals deck. Severity colours follow PAGASA's own rainfall-warning convention, so residents do not have to learn a second colour language, and every severity state carries an icon and a text label, never colour alone. Sourced figures are cited on the screen that shows them. Live sensor readings are marked like this, because no sensor is deployed and no reading of the real river exists to quote.

No sensors deployed yet

This panel fills once PULSE units are installed on Del Rosario, Sta. Rita and Kalaklan. Until then there is nothing real to show here.

Why SAR, not optical. Monsoon cloud blocks optical satellite imagery exactly when scanning matters most, so Sentinel-1 radar is the primary feed during active monsoon windows. A drone flyover is triggered automatically whenever a PULSE sensor crosses its warning threshold.
